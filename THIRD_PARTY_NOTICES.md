# Third-party components

The repository's MIT license applies to its original code, not dependencies.
Dependencies retain their own licenses and notices. Setup downloads dependencies
instead of vendoring installed environments or native binaries into this repo.

CalculiX: https://www.dhondt.de/ and https://github.com/calculix/CalculiX-Windows.
Native distribution licenses/notices stay with the downloaded pinned archive.
Other optional packages include Gmsh, OpenCascade/OCP, VTK/PyVista, Qt/PySide6,
NumPy and SciPy. See their installed license metadata before redistribution.
